DTF FAST PREP
Open your artwork and click Run DTF Fast Prep. A new document is created at the "Scale to" size (300 DPI); your original is not changed. Then adjust live: • Color Knockout removes the shirt color smoothly, so the shirt shows through. • Levels: alpha (0–255) at or below Black becomes clear, at or above White becomes solid; drag Midtone left to make soft areas more opaque. • Output Levels: lower High below 255 to force solid areas into dots. • Boost Shadow: keeps more of faint, soft areas. • Fast Halftone turns everything semi-transparent into solid dots (Edit Halftones for frequency, angle, shape). • Post Cleanup removes specks too small to print. • Original / Shirt / Alpha / Mask switch the view. While editing you stay on the new document. Apply adds the DTF Fast Prep layer with the halftone as its layer mask (Original is kept hidden below); Cancel closes the new document.
